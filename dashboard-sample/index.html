Takeaway

Revenue is highly concentrated. The United Kingdom drives 84.6 percent of revenue, and the top 10 customers drive 14.4 percent. Revenue also peaks late in the year, with Nov 2011 the strongest month at $1,509,496.33, which is 14.2 percent of the full year.

Recommendation Protect the United Kingdom base and the top 10 customers first, and plan inventory and staffing for the Sep to Nov peak, since that is when demand is highest.

Data from UCI Online Retail, a UK based online retailer covering Dec 2010 to Dec 2011. The file holds 541,909 order lines. After cleaning, 530,104 lines remain. Cleaning removes lines with a negative quantity, which are cancellations, and lines with a zero unit price, which carry no sales value.

No real employer or client data is used anywhere in this project. The data is public, and the analysis is my own.
